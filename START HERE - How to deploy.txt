WORD SHUFFLE  -  KNITTING EDITION  🧶
=====================================

WHAT CHANGED
------------
The whole game now wears the same knitting look as your platform:
 - Knitted-fabric background (tiny rows of stitches) in soft wool colours
 - Every button is a little yarn ball with a stitched ring
 - Every letter box is a knitted patch with a stitched border, and each one
   is a different yarn colour (mustard, moss, terracotta, denim, rose, lavender)
 - Solved words turn moss-green
 - Timer bar looks like twisted yarn
 - Stitched (dashed) borders on the leaderboard, chat, winner window, menus
   and settings screens
 - THEMES (tap the theme button on the top bar to switch). The list has
   two groups:
     1) 🧶 KNITTED BACKGROUND - the 6 wool themes from your platform
        (Cream, Sky Blue, Meadow, Blossom, Lavender, Honey) + Night Wool (dark)
     2) 🎨 PLAIN BACKGROUND - ALL your previous themes (Cotton Candy, Peach
        Bunny, Minty Fresh, Dark, Light, Cream, Sky Blue, Meadow Green,
        Blossom Pink, Lavender Violet, Honey Gold, Bubblegum, Candy Pink,
        Aurora, Sunset, Ocean, Midnight, Neon Arcade, Coffee).
        Same colours as before, with the knitting buttons, stitched borders
        and yarn letter boxes, but a smooth plain colour background.
 - New puzzle category: KNITTING & YARN
 - Yarn-ball icon on the browser tab

You do NOT need to change anything in the code.


HOW TO DEPLOY (same way as before)
----------------------------------
1. Unzip / open this folder "word-shuffle" on your computer.

2. GitHub:
   - Open your existing WORD SHUFFLE repository on github.com
     (or make a new one: + > New repository, do NOT tick "Add a README").
   - Click "Add file" > "Upload files".
   - Select EVERYTHING inside the word-shuffle folder (Ctrl+A) and drag it
     onto the page. Drag the folders themselves (client, data, tools).
   - Click the green "Commit changes" button.

3. Render.com:
   - If you already have WORD SHUFFLE on Render, it redeploys by itself
     after you commit. Wait until it says "Live".
   - If it is new: New + > Web Service > pick your repository, then set
        Build Command:  npm install
        Start Command:  npm start
     and add these under Environment:
        TIKTOK_USERNAME  = your TikTok name (no @)
        TIKTOK_SIGN_API_KEY = your Euler key
     Then click "Create Web Service".

4. Open your Render link on your phone. You should see the cream wool
   background with yarn-coloured letter boxes.


IF YOU STILL SEE THE OLD LOOK
-----------------------------
 - Refresh the page (pull down), or close and reopen the browser tab.
 - The first time it opens, the game clears the OLD custom colours
   saved on your phone, so the knitting look shows up. Your chosen theme is kept. Your other settings
   (round time, title text, toolbar buttons, etc.) are kept.

OPTIONAL LOOK SETTINGS
----------------------
Settings (gear button) > Look > Letter boxes:
  "Multicolour yarn boxes" - turn OFF if you want all boxes in one wool
  colour (the theme colour) instead.
